## Attach and export

1. **Copy Report.** Click it in Ezio. Paste the complete export after your workflow and decision card: input, findings, claim explanations, source links, and available **Analysis started / Exported at** timestamps. Leave unknown dates unknown. Plain text is fine if readable.
2. **Export one PDF.** Only the workflow is limited to one page; all appendix material is outside that limit. Open it: is everything readable, complete, and linked? A score screenshot or account-restricted report link is not a substitute.
3. **Submit by 3:00 PM PT, October 10, 2026**, through the [submission form](https://forms.gle/Q9HSk2MUS1HywiqS6). Optional video is separate; no live presentation.

**Working on paper?** Put a readable photo of your page before the appendix; ask for assembly help.

**Access failed?** Ask an organizer rather than repeatedly retrying.
